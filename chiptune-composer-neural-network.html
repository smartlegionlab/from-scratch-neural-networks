How to Use

  1. Play unlocks Audio and starts live composition.
  2. Record: REC - Stop - Download (.wav); Reset clears.
  3. Master Volume and Mute All / Unmute All; each channel has its own Mute.
  4. Key, Tempo (40+), Temperature, Lead Density, Model influence, Echo.
  5. The network picks the melody and a per-bar rhythm groove for bass + drums.
  6. Copy Stats / Export / Import.

About

Chiptune Composer Neural Network
version 1.7.0

A from-scratch MLP (53 -> 48 -> 12 + rhythm) in pure JavaScript. No libraries, no samples.

The network has two shared-hidden heads: it predicts the next pitch class and, per bar, one of four rhythm grooves for bass + drums. Trained by mini-batch SGD / Adam with cross-entropy, weight decay and clipping. Melody is locked to the chosen key / scale.

An educational demo of live music generation: every note is synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API, and the piece stays in key and follows a chord progression.

6 channels: square lead (bright) with a dotted-eighth feedback delay + lowpass, square arpeggio, moving triangle bass, noise drums (kick / snare / hi-hat), second lead an octave down, and a sustained triangle pad. Records to 16-bit PCM .wav.
